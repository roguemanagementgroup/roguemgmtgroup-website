Profile

Governance first, then automation

Stephen Zeitvogel is the founder and chief architect of Rogue Management Group LLC, a systems architecture and governance firm in Charlotte, North Carolina. He designed RogueOS, a deterministic, locally executing autonomous agent governance framework, and authored its architecture under two USPTO provisional patent applications filed in December 2025.

His work is grounded in production security and live forensics, not theory. Rogue Management Group was built directly out of real-time incident response, where he isolated and documented intrusions while keeping operations running. He works as a forward deployed engineer who maps execution paths, failure modes, and governance gaps first, then translates business goals into technical specifications he validates in production.

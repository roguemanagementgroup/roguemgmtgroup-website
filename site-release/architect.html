Biography

Systems architecture built around accountable action.

Stephen Zeitvogel is the founder and Chief Architect of Rogue Management Group, LLC in Charlotte, North Carolina. His work centers on governance for autonomous AI systems: defining who has authority, how that authority is bounded, and what evidence an execution environment should preserve.

His résumé describes experience in technical consulting, systems infrastructure, cybersecurity investigation, vulnerability analysis, evidence preservation and enterprise-facing technical communication. It also describes work at American Airlines in time-sensitive, checklist-driven ground operations.

At Rogue Management Group, his authored frameworks include Builder's Permit™, RogueOS™ and the later Shadow Rogue™ operator-shell concept. The work emphasizes a division between probabilistic model planning and deterministic execution authorization.

Selected focus areas

  • AI governance and autonomous-system authority boundaries
  • Control-plane architecture and permissioned agent execution
  • Digital forensics, audit trails and evidence preservation
  • Business-to-engineering translation and implementation strategy

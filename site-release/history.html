A record of development

From the original framework to Shadow Rogue.

Selected dates and milestones drawn from RMG project materials and the investor documents supplied for this site. A chronology of documented claims is not independent proof of technical priority, patent validity, or deployment readiness.

May 2025

Early architecture and company formation

RMG materials place early local autonomy and Builder's Permit governance concepts in May–June 2025.

June 22, 2025

First cited provisional application

RMG documents identify USPTO provisional application 63/828,137. Filing details and current status should be checked against official USPTO records before reliance.

August 14, 2025

Second cited provisional application

RMG documents identify USPTO provisional application 63/864,057, concerning the autonomous governance architecture.

December 14, 2025

Rogue Enterprise Framework v1.0

Materials identify the framework's write-ahead logging, policy-bound execution, local trust, and deterministic replay concepts.

2026

Pre-Execution Authority & eight primitives

The 2026 materials further articulate the eight-primitive specification and Pre-Execution Authority terminology. They build on RMG's documented 2025 governance framework; this timeline entry does not assign first conception of deterministic governance to 2026.

September 2026

Shadow Rogue operator shell

RMG's September materials describe a pointer-anchored desktop/browser visual operator with explicit approval states and bounded automation.

October 2026

Commercial and investor packaging

RMG prepared investor materials outlining a governance kernel MVP, an enterprise pilot strategy, and strategic partnership options.

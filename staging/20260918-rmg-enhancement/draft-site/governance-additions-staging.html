Governance additions under research

These are research directions and prototype topics for validation, not shipped capabilities, legal claims, patent claims, or customer assurances. Any future implementation requires threat modeling, independent testing, documented scope, and review against the target environment.

01 | Hardware-backed enclaves

Explore hardware-isolated execution for sensitive agent tasks. Prototype work must document supported hardware, trust assumptions, side-channel limits, recovery behavior, and what is not protected.

02 | Agent mTLS and interoperability

Evaluate mutual-TLS identity and interoperable agent contracts across controlled systems. Compatibility, certificate lifecycle, revocation, and authorization semantics remain validation work.

03 | Formal verification

Investigate proofs for selected policy and runtime invariants. A proof for a model or component would not establish safety for the complete deployed system.

04 | Policy compilation

Prototype compiling human-readable governance rules into reviewable enforcement artifacts. Semantics, ambiguous requirements, versioning, and fail-closed behavior require test coverage and operator review.

05 | Offline attestation

Explore offline evidence bundles and later verification for constrained environments. Freshness, key custody, tamper evidence, clock assumptions, and resynchronization remain open validation limits.

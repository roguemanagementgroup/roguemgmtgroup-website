Find your clockand punch it.

There used to be a clock on the wall. You slid your card in on Friday and it made a sound. That sound meant you earned something. It meant somebody was proud of you and you were going out that night with money in your pocket and nothing hanging over you.

I was a framer. Sixteen-ounce Estwing, walking a top plate, back before most of us had a nail gun. Nobody handed me anything, and I have never felt better than I felt on those Fridays.

Four years of debt

  • Forty-four million Americans currently carry one point six trillion dollars in student loans.
  • You pay first and find out later whether the credential is worth anything.
  • The entry-level version of that job is the first thing being automated.
  • You graduate at twenty-two owing more than a first house cost your parents.

Four years of leverage

  • You get paid while you learn. Apprenticeship is a job, not a bill.
  • It cannot be offshored. Conduit gets pulled where the building is.
  • The demand is already here. Grid, data centers, plants, defense. All of it needs hands now.
  • Six figures is normal, not exceptional, for licensed trades with a few years in.
field: the edge

Command it. Do not compete with it.

Every one of these jobs is about to come with software attached. The only question that matters is who that software answers to. We built ours so it answers to the person holding the permit.

01

You issue, it executes

You write the permit. The agent works inside it and comes back for a new one when the job changes. You are not labor racing a machine. You are the licensed party signing for one.

02

Your record, your hardware

Everything it does is written to a log you hold, on a machine you own. When somebody disputes what happened on the job, you are the one with the file. Ask anyone who has ever needed one.

03

A credential that stacks

Running and authorizing a governed agent sits on top of your trade instead of replacing it. The electrician who can issue permits to his own tooling is not the one who gets automated out.

04

Built here, held here

Local-first by design. The record does not leave the country and no vendor can revoke your access to the proof of your own work.

Somebody already set the money aside

The mikeroweWORKS Foundation is awarding ten million dollars in work ethic scholarships this year. No GPA requirement. No test scores. They cover two-year programs and shorter, including tuition, books, and tools. Since 2008 they have put more than sixteen million dollars into the hands of over twenty-six hundred people. It is the largest trade school scholarship fund in the country, and applications are open right now.

01

Read and sign the S.W.E.A.T. Pledge

Their application is built around it. It is about showing up, staying late, and taking pride in the work. If that already describes you, this part is free.

02

Answer four short questions

Written, not long. They are reading for work ethic, not for prose.

03

Shoot a sixty-second video

Phone camera is fine. Face a window, never have one behind you. Say who you are, what trade you want, and why you will finish. Sixty seconds, one take, no music.

04
